# OECD-enhanced EOM macro comparison

- Comparison year: 2024
- Destinations: 17
- Baseline: World Bank six-component equal-weight EOM macro proxy.
- OECD signal: A.1 inflow rate, A.4 foreign-born share, A.6 nationality-acquisition share.
- Blend: 80% standardized baseline attractiveness + 20% standardized OECD ecosystem signal.
- Sensitivity rankings: 10%, 20%, 30% OECD weights.
- A.3 asylum requests are excluded from attractiveness scoring and retained as contextual pressure; A.5 is excluded to avoid overlapping stock measures.
- This is exploratory and not a calibrated migration probability or an individual recommendation.
